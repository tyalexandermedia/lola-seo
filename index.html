Straight answers.

Do I need to be an athlete to train with you?
No. You need to want to move well and train hard. We start from where you are today and build strength, mobility and conditioning from there.
Where are you located?
I'm based in St. Pete and serve all of Pinellas County. Basketball sessions are on the court, and I text you the exact location when you claim a spot. For strength and conditioning I come to your home or gym.
How much does coaching cost?
Basketball training is semi-private: 3 to 4 players at set times, $280 for a 4-week package at twice a week ($35 a session), or $40 for a single session. One-on-one is $260 for four weekly sessions ($65 a session), or $75 for a single session. Strength and conditioning starts with an intro 4-pack: 4 sessions for $240, then $85 per session.
How do I start?
Tap Get my price, pick basketball or strength, choose a day and I'll text you to confirm. It takes about 30 seconds and there's no payment on the page.
