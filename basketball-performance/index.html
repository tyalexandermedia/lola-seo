Why It Matters

The game asks a lot of your body.

I've seen it as a player and as a coach: skill work only goes as far as the body carrying it. Stopping, cutting, jumping and landing are skills too, and so is being strong enough to play your game late in the fourth.

  • Skills — on-court clinics and sessions, coming soon
  • Movement — deceleration, change of direction, jumping and landing
  • Strength & power — a stronger base to play from
  • Conditioning — built around how the game is actually played
  • Coaching — direct feedback from a coach who runs a team
What We Train

The off-court program

Right now my 1:1 basketball work is off-court: strength, movement and conditioning that go alongside your skill work. On-court skills clinics are coming soon.

01

Strength

Lower-body power, core strength and a base that transfers to the court.

02

Movement

Deceleration, change of direction and jumping and landing mechanics.

03

Conditioning

Work capacity built around the stop-and-go demands of a game.

04

Mobility

Ankle, hip and knee mobility work so you can get into the positions the game asks for.

Honest note: training builds strength, movement and conditioning. It doesn't promise scholarships, playing time or guaranteed results — that part is earned on the court.
