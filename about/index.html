About Coach Ty

I train people the way I wish I'd been trained.

I'm Ty — Coach Ty to most people. I'm a personal trainer in St. Petersburg and Head Coach with Florida Rain. I coach movement, mobility and strength for athletes who want to move well and train hard.

Basketball is my athletic foundation. I play it and I coach it, and it shaped how I think about movement.

HYROX is my current journey. I started as a non-runner and completed my first solo Open in 1:29:59. That race exposed my limitations: ankle mobility, calf capacity and running under fatigue. So I'm working on them and sharing what I learn. Found my weakness. Fixing it in public. Follow the Road to Miami →

That's also how I coach. Ankles, hips and knees get real attention, mobility gets trained with load on it, and the program is built for your body, not copied from someone else's. See my athlete profile →

Away from the gym, faith and family come first.
