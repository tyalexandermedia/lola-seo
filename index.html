About Ty

An eye for movement, properties and the places people love.

Ty Alexander is a Tampa Bay-based, FAA Part 107-certified drone pilot and content creator focused on one thing: making a location look worth showing up for. The work leans into movement — the sweep over the waterline, the reveal of a building, the energy of a room — and translates it into video, vertical clips and immersive 360 tours businesses can actually use.

From gyms and restaurants to resorts, commercial sites and the properties in between, the goal is the same: give people the full picture of a place before they ever walk in. Local, hands-on, and obsessed with getting the shot right.
