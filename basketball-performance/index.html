What a session looks like.

Skill work only goes as far as the body carrying it, so every session trains both.

Skills

Shooting, handles, finishing and footwork for the player's position.

Strength and power

Lower-body power and core strength that carry over to the game.

Movement and mobility

Stopping, cutting, jumping and landing, plus ankle, knee and hip mobility.

Conditioning

Work capacity built around the stop-and-go of a real game.

Honest note: training builds skill, strength and conditioning. It doesn't promise scholarships or playing time. That part is earned on the court.
