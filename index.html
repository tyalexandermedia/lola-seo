Questions

Straight answers

Do I need to be an athlete to train with you?
No. Most people I coach are adults who want to get stronger, move better and improve conditioning for real life. "Everyday athlete" means we train you like an athlete — strength, movement and conditioning — scaled to where you are today.
What is semi-private coaching?
You train in a small group on a structured, progressive program while I coach you individually — correcting form, adjusting loads and tracking your progress. You get personal guidance without the full cost of one-on-one.
Where are you located?
I coach in St. Petersburg, Florida and serve the greater Tampa Bay area. Apply for coaching and I'll follow up with current session details based on your goals and availability.
How much does coaching cost?
Semi-private coaching starts at $249/month and one-on-one private coaching starts at $400/month. Your final recommendation depends on your goals, schedule and availability — we sort that out after you apply.
How do I start?
Apply for coaching — it takes about two minutes. I review every application personally and follow up to talk through your goals and the best way to train together.

Ready to Train With Purpose?

Tell me your goals and how you want to train. I read every application personally and follow up to build the right plan for you.
