Which option should I choose?
To start now, pick basketball training or strength and conditioning and get your price for the intro 4-pack. For private 1-on-1, ask about current availability. If small-group training fits you better, join the semi-private waitlist and I'll reach out once the location and schedule are confirmed.
Do you program mobility and conditioning, or just lifting?
All of it. Mobility, strength, athletic movement and conditioning live in one program. Ankles, knees and hips get specific attention.
What does it cost?
Intro 4-packs are $160 for basketball and $240 for strength. Private 1-on-1 starts at $400/month, by inquiry. Semi-private is on a waitlist with a planned price of $249/month.
Is semi-private coaching open now?
Not yet. It's a waitlist. I'm confirming the Pinellas County location and the schedule, and there's no start date to announce. Joining the waitlist doesn't cost anything or commit you to anything.
Where and when do sessions happen?
Across Pinellas County, from St. Pete to Palm Harbor. Basketball training runs mainly in Clearwater. The specific location and schedule for semi-private aren't confirmed yet. For private 1:1, we work that out when you inquire.
I'm not in great shape yet. Is that okay?
That's exactly who this is for. Everything is scaled to where you are today and progressed from there. You don't need to get "in shape" before you start. Starting is how you get in shape.
