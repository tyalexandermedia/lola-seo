I train people the way I wish I'd been trained.

I'm Ty, Coach Ty to most people. I'm Head Coach with Florida Rain and I've coached since 2012, training players and adults across Pinellas County, from St. Pete to Palm Harbor. I coach skills, movement and strength for athletes who want to move well and train hard.

Build the athlete under the player.

Basketball first. HYROX now.

Basketball is my athletic foundation. I play it and I coach it, and it shaped how I think about movement.

HYROX is my current journey. I started as a non-runner and completed my first solo Open in 1:29:59. That race exposed my limitations: ankle mobility, calf capacity and running under fatigue. So I'm working on them and sharing what I learn. Found my weakness. Fixing it in public. Follow the Road to Miami.

That's also how I coach. Ankles, knees and hips get real attention, mobility gets trained with load on it, and the program is built for your body, not copied from someone else's. See my athlete profile.

Away from the gym, faith and family come first.
