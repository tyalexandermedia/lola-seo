How it works.

We start at the floor and build up. Strength comes after we know how you move.

Check

Session one starts with ankles, knees and hips. You get your numbers and what they mean for your training.

Build

Strength work matched to what we found: squats, hinges, pushes, pulls and carries at loads you can control.

Condition

Work capacity built in, so you can keep up with your sport, your job and your life.

Retest

We repeat the check so you can see what changed.

Honest note: this is coaching, not physical therapy. If something hurts, see a doctor or physical therapist first. Try the free movement check yourself.
