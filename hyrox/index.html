The same things I'm working on.

HYROX is eight 1 km runs with a functional station between each. It asks for running, strength and conditioning at the same time. This is how I break the training down.

Running

Building consistency and an aerobic base, especially if you're not a runner yet. I wasn't.

Functional strength

Sled push and pull, lunges, carries and wall balls, practiced until they hold up when you're tired.

Ankles and calves

Mobility and calf capacity work, the limiters my first race exposed.

Movement under fatigue

Practicing good positions late in a session, when it's hardest to keep them.

For people starting where I started.

  • Lifters and court-sport athletes who want to try a first HYROX
  • Non-runners who need to build running into their week
  • Anyone who wants hybrid training, no race required
  • People who'd rather train with a coach who's honest about what he's still figuring out

Honest note: I'm not a HYROX specialist and I don't promise finish times. I have one race, a coaching background, and a plan I'm testing on myself first.
