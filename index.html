    Straight answers.

    Still have a question? Text me.

    (727) 300-6573
    Do I need to be an athlete to train with you?
    No. You need to want to move well and train hard. We start from where you are today and build strength, mobility and conditioning from there.
    Where are you located?
    I'm based in St. Pete and serve all of Pinellas County. Sessions are at a St. Pete gym or in your home, and I confirm the location when you book. Adults can also train at their own gym.
    How much does coaching cost?
    Everything comes in packs. Semi-private basketball for 2 to 4 players is 8 sessions for $280 ($35 a session) or 4 for $160. One-on-one is 4 sessions for $260 or 8 for $480. Strength and conditioning is a 4-pack: 4 sessions for $200.
    How do I start?
    Tap Get my price, pick basketball or strength, choose a day and I'll text you to confirm. It takes about 30 seconds and there's no payment on the page.
    What should my player bring?
    Basketball shoes and water. I bring the equipment.
    What ages do you train?
    I coach middle school and high school players, and adults train with me in strength and conditioning. Tell me the player's age and level when you book.
    What if we don't love the first session?
    Then your next session is free. Love your first session or your next one's free.
    How long do we have to use a pack?
    Use an 8-pack within 10 weeks. That keeps training steady enough to see real change between the first and last session.
    What if we need to reschedule or cancel?
    Text me as early as you can and we'll find another time. I go over the details when you book.

    Claim your spot.
