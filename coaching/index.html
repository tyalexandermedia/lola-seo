Which option should I choose?
Players who train well with others do great in semi-private. Players who need all of my attention go one-on-one. Adults and everyday athletes start with the strength and conditioning 4-pack. Pick one, choose a day, and I'll text you to confirm.
Do you program mobility and conditioning, or just lifting?
All of it. Mobility, strength, athletic movement and conditioning live in one program. Ankles, knees and hips get specific attention.
What does it cost?
Everything comes in packs. Semi-private basketball is 8 sessions for $280 or 4 for $160. One-on-one is 4 sessions for $260 or 8 for $480. Strength and conditioning is $200 for a 4-pack.
Is semi-private coaching open now?
Yes. Semi-private basketball is open now: 2 to 4 players at set times. 8 sessions for $280, or 4 for $160.
Where and when do sessions happen?
Based in St. Pete, serving all of Pinellas County. Sessions are at a St. Pete gym or in your home, and I confirm the location and times when you book. Adults can also train at their own gym.
I'm not in great shape yet. Is that okay?
That's exactly who this is for. Everything is scaled to where you are today and progressed from there. You don't need to get "in shape" before you start. Starting is how you get in shape.
