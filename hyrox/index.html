The Program

Train every side of the race

HYROX is eight 1 km runs with a functional station between each. That takes a specific kind of fitness — and a plan that builds all of it together instead of leaving gaps.

01

Running engine

Aerobic base and pacing so the runs don't wreck you before the stations.

02

Functional strength

Sled push/pull, lunges, carries and wall balls — strong and repeatable under fatigue.

03

Conditioning

Work capacity that holds up across the whole hour, not just the first station.

04

Transitions & pacing

The race strategy most people skip — where good times are won and lost.

Who It's For

  • First-timers who want to finish strong and enjoy race day
  • Returning athletes chasing a faster time or a PR
  • Anyone who just wants a serious hybrid engine — no race required

Questions

Good to know

Do I need to have done a HYROX before?
Not at all. Plenty of people start here to train for their first one — or just to get genuinely fit. We scale everything to where you are and build from there.
Is this only for the race, or for fitness too?
Both. The hybrid engine — run, lift, keep going — is one of the best ways to be fit for real life. A race just gives you a date to train toward if you want one.
Do you have the equipment for the stations?
We program around what's available and replicate the demands of each station. Apply and I'll walk you through exactly how we'll train for your setup and goals.

Ready to Train With Purpose?

Whether it's your first HYROX or your fastest, tell me your goals and race date and I'll build the plan to get you there.
