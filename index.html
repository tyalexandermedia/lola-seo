The Ground-Up Method.

Basketball players and adults go through the same four steps. We start at the floor: ankles, then knees, then hips.

  1. Check

    Session one is a movement check. You get your numbers and what they mean.

  2. Build

    Strength and skill work matched to what the check showed.

  3. Condition

    Work capacity for your game, your job and your life.

  4. Retest

    We repeat the check so you can see what changed.

Athlete turned coach.

Basketball is my athletic foundation. I play it and I coach it as Head Coach with Florida Rain, and it shaped how I think about movement: ball handling, coordination and athleticism.

I coach through form, small adjustments and leading by example, not by yelling. Ankles, knees and hips get real attention, and the program is built for your body.

Straight answers.

Do I need to be an athlete to train with you?
No. You need to want to move well and train hard. We start from where you are today and build strength, mobility and conditioning from there.
Where are you located?
I'm based in St. Pete and serve all of Pinellas County. Basketball sessions are on the court, and I text you the exact location when you claim a spot. For strength and conditioning I come to your home or gym.
How much does coaching cost?
Basketball training is semi-private: 3 to 4 players at set times, $280 for a 4-week package at twice a week ($35 a session), or $40 for a single session. One-on-one is $200 for four weekly sessions ($50 a session), or $60 for a single session. Strength and conditioning starts with an intro 4-pack: 4 sessions for $240, then $85 per session.
