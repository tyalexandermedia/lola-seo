FAQ

Good questions.

Are you licensed to fly commercially?

Yes — Ty is an FAA Part 107-certified remote pilot. Every commercial flight is planned and flown under FAA rules, with airspace, weather and site safety checked before takeoff.

Which businesses is drone content best for?

Gyms and fitness facilities, restaurants and waterfront venues, resorts and vacation rentals, commercial properties, property managers, premium home-service projects, and any local business with a space worth showing.

What do I actually receive?

Depending on scope: aerial video capture, an edited hero video, vertical social clips, selected aerial photos and web-ready delivery. The Property Visibility Package adds 360 capture and Google Business Profile-ready assets.

How do weather and airspace affect the shoot?

We plan every flight around FAA airspace rules, local site conditions, weather and safety before we launch. Some locations need advance scheduling, and we'll reschedule rather than risk an unsafe or low-quality flight.

How much does a drone shoot cost?

Drone Content Sessions start at $450 and the Property Visibility Package starts at $950. Final pricing depends on location, property size, flight requirements, deliverables and editing scope.
