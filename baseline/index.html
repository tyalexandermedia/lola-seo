Build the athlete under the player.

Free movement check

4 quick tests at home, about 3 minutes. Tap what happened and get your score plus one drill.

Test 1 of 4: Ankles

Knee to wall

  1. Barefoot, toes one hand-width from a wall.
  2. Keep your heel down and push your knee forward to touch the wall.
  3. Try both sides.

Pass: your knee touches the wall on both sides with the heel flat.

Ankle bend lets your knee travel forward in every squat, landing and defensive stance.

How did it go?

Test 2 of 4: Knees

Step-down

  1. Stand on one leg on the bottom stair, a hand near the rail.
  2. Slowly lower the other heel to tap the floor, then stand back up.
  3. Do 5 on each leg.

Pass: 5 slow reps each leg, knee over your middle toes, hips level.

Every stride, cut and landing happens on one leg. This shows whether you control it.

How did it go?

Test 3 of 4: Hips

Deep squat hold

  1. Feet about shoulder-width, toes turned out a little.
  2. Sit as low as you can with your heels down.
  3. Hold for 10 seconds.

Pass: hips below your knees, heels flat, for the full 10 seconds.

When the hips don't open, the knees and lower back do the work for them.

How did it go?

Test 4 of 4: Balance

One-leg stand

  1. Stand on one leg with your hands on your hips.
  2. Hold for 30 seconds without touching down.
  3. Switch legs.

Pass: 30 seconds on each leg without the other foot touching down.

Balance is control. It is what keeps you strong in awkward positions.

How did it go?
