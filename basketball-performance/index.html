The Ground-Up Method.

Every player goes through the same four steps, starting at the floor.

  1. Check

    Session one: ankles, knees and hips, plus vertical jump and a three-quarter-court sprint. We write the numbers down.

  2. Build

    Skills and strength work matched to what the check showed and the player's position.

  3. Condition

    Work capacity built around the stop-and-go of a real game.

  4. Retest

    Session four: the same tests again, and the report card with both sets of numbers.

What a session looks like.

Skill work only goes as far as the body carrying it, so every session trains both.

Skills

Shooting, handles, finishing and footwork for the player's position.

Strength and power

Lower-body power and core strength that carry over to the game.

Movement and mobility

Stopping, cutting, jumping and landing, plus ankle, knee and hip mobility.

Conditioning

Work capacity built around the stop-and-go of a real game.
