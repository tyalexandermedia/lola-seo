Straight answers.

Do I need to be an athlete to train with you?
No. You need to want to move well and train hard. We start from where you are today and build strength, mobility and conditioning from there.
What is semi-private coaching?
Small-group training on a structured program, with individual coaching on form, loads and progress. It's a waitlist right now while the Pinellas County location and schedule are being confirmed.
Where are you located?
I coach across Pinellas County, from St. Pete to Palm Harbor. Basketball training runs mainly in Clearwater, where I have court access. The location and schedule for semi-private coaching are still being confirmed. For private 1:1 coaching, contact me for current availability.
How much does coaching cost?
Basketball training has an intro special of 4 sessions for $160, then $50 per session. Semi-private coaching is on a waitlist with a planned price of $249/month. Private 1:1 coaching starts at $400/month. Contact me for current availability.
How do I start?
Apply for coaching. It takes about two minutes. I review every application personally and follow up to talk through your goals and what's currently available.
