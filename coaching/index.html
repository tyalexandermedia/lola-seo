Which option should I choose?
If you want to start now, ask about private 1:1 and I'll tell you about current availability. If small-group training fits you better, join the semi-private waitlist and I'll reach out once the location and schedule are confirmed.
Do you program mobility and conditioning, or just lifting?
All of it. Mobility, strength, athletic movement and conditioning live in one program. Ankles, hips and knees get specific attention.
What does it cost?
Semi-private is on a waitlist with a planned price of $249/month. Private 1:1 starts at $400/month. Contact me for current availability.
Is semi-private coaching open now?
Not yet. It's a waitlist. I'm confirming the St. Petersburg location and the schedule, and there's no start date to announce. Joining the waitlist doesn't cost anything or commit you to anything.
Where and when do sessions happen?
In St. Petersburg, Florida. The specific location and schedule for semi-private aren't confirmed yet. For private 1:1, we work that out when you inquire.
I'm not in great shape yet. Is that okay?
That's exactly who this is for. Everything is scaled to where you are today and progressed from there. You don't need to get "in shape" before you start. Starting is how you get in shape.
